📚 Himachal Pradesh – Static GK

🏛️ Formation & Administration

  • Statehood: 25 January 1971 (18th state of India)
  • Capital: Shimla (Summer), Dharamsala (Winter)
  • High Court: Shimla
  • Rajya Sabha Seats: 3 | Lok Sabha Seats: 4
  • State Assembly: 68 seats
  • First CM: Dr. Y.S. Parmar
  • Total Districts: 12 | Divisions: 3
  • Tehsils: 172 | Development Blocks: 78

🌿 Geography & Environment

  • Total Area: 55,673 km² (18th largest state)
  • Highest Peak: Reo Purgyil (6,816 m) – Kinnaur
  • 5 Rivers: Sutlej, Beas, Ravi, Chenab, Yamuna
  • State Tree: Deodar Cedar
  • State Flower: Pink Rhododendron (Burans)
  • State Animal: Snow Leopard
  • State Bird: Western Tragopan (Jujurana)
  • Largest Glacier: Bara Shigri (Lahaul-Spiti)

🏔️ National Parks & Wildlife

  • Great Himalayan NP (UNESCO WHS, 2014) – Kullu
  • Pin Valley NP – Spiti (Cold Desert biome)
  • Khirganga NP – Kullu
  • Simbalbara NP – Sirmaur (est. 2010)
  • Inderkilla NP – Kullu
  • Renuka WL Sanctuary – Sirmaur (largest natural lake)
  • Chail WL Sanctuary – Shimla
  • Shikari Devi WL Sanctuary – Mandi

⚡ Economy & Power

  • Hydropower Potential: 27,436 MW (3rd highest in India)
  • Bhakra Dam: Gobind Sagar Lake (Satluj, Bilaspur)
  • Largest Hydro Project: Nathpa Jhakri (1,500 MW)
  • HP ranks 1st: Apple production in India
  • Apple Economy: ₹3,500 crore annually
  • IT Park: Shimla, Waknaghat
  • Pharma hub: Baddi (Asia's largest pharma cluster)
  • Salt Mines: Drang & Guma (Mandi district)

🛣️ Infrastructure & Records

  • Atal Tunnel: World's longest highway tunnel at altitude (9.02 km)
  • Kalka-Shimla Railway: UNESCO World Heritage (2008)
  • Airports: Shimla, Kullu-Manali (Bhuntar), Dharamsala (Gaggal)
  • Hamirpur: Highest road density in India
  • HRTC: State road transport (3,100+ buses)
  • Chail: World's highest cricket ground (2,444 m)
  • Road Network: 28,208 km total

🎭 Culture & Heritage

  • Kullu Dussehra: 7-day International Festival
  • Nati: World's largest folk dance (Guinness Record)
  • Minjar Fair: Chamba (maize celebration)
  • Kangra Painting: 18th century recognized art form
  • GI Tags: Kullu Shawl, Kinnauri Shawl, Kangra Tea, Chamba Rumal
  • Bhuri Singh Museum: Chamba (est. 1908)
  • Pragpur (Kangra): India's 1st Heritage Village
  • Chittkul (Kinnaur): Last inhabited village on Indo-Tibet road

🏫 Education & Health

  • HP University: Shimla (est. 1970)
  • IIT Mandi – est. 2009 | NIT Hamirpur – est. 1986
  • IGMC: Premier medical college, Shimla
  • Dr. YS Parmar UHF: Solan (Horticulture & Forestry)
  • HP Board of School Education: HPBOSE, Dharamsala
  • Literacy Rate: 82.8% (M:89.5%, F:75.9%)
  • Hamirpur: Most literate district (89.01%)
  • Sex Ratio: 972 females per 1,000 males

🏆 HP Firsts & Unique Facts

  • 2nd open-defecation free state: 2016
  • First smoke-free state in India
  • Least corrupt state (CMS India survey, 2017)
  • First state with 100% electrification: 1988
  • Tabo Monastery (996 AD): oldest continuously operating in India
  • Gobind Sagar: 2nd largest artificial reservoir in Asia
  • Chamba: only state in N. India with documented history from 500 AD
  • Una: only HP district with broad-gauge railway
